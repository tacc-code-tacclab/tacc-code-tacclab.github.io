# The Northern Kthulu and the Silent Vessel — version 13

Self-contained build (`index.html`). Earlier versions stay where they are: `../northern-kthulu-v12/`,
`../northern-kthulu-v11/`, `../northern-kthulu-v10/`, `../northern-kthulu/` (version 9).

New in 13: the helicopter can be boarded again during the duel with Nyarlathotep — its guns finish him quickly, but
every round that misses hits the ship, and too many destroy her; on foot he is gentler; the moon and the sun no longer
show together; polar bears walk about on their floes; the white slab on the laboratory floor is gone; pickups that
could not be reached or hung in mid-air are fixed; picking up a card or key says what it opens and a locked door says
what opened it.

Languages: Italiano (default), English, Español, Français, Deutsch — Settings → Language.
PC: WASD · mouse · left mouse / R / Q / Enter fire · T reload · C crouch (X toggles) · Space jump · E use · 1–7 weapons.
Helicopter: WASD fly · Space climb · C descend · hold fire.
Phone / tablet: landscape, left thumb moves, right thumb looks, on-screen buttons.
