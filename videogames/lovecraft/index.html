LOVECRAFT II

The expert chart — books, rites, saltes, and the thing under the seal
… VISITORS

Everything you build is kept: the world writes itself down every 45 seconds and waits for you. A chart nobody visits for 15 days is deleted on its own, so nothing of yours is left cluttering the machine. Export any chart to a file and that copy never expires.

Five countries that do not look alike: green basalt drowning in black water, a grey tide shore, olive uplands scabbed with blasted heath, a white plateau under black spires, a violet wood lit from the ground. They meet every two or three hundred paces and the book names them as you cross.

New in this chart: bound books to find and read, a nine block circle to lay by hand, essential saltes that raise something to fight beside you, the elder sign that turns them aside, and a red seal in the deep vaults that you should think about twice before breaking.

There is no edge. Walk in one direction for an hour and the world keeps being made in front of you, coast after coast, plateau after plateau, and none of it was waiting for you. Dig it up, carry it, build with it. The things that live here were building long before, and their angles are wrong on purpose.
