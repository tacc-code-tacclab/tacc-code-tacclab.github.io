# The Northern Kthulu and the Silent Vessel — version 11

Self-contained build (`index.html`). Version 10 stays at `../northern-kthulu-v10/`, version 9 at `../northern-kthulu/`.

New in 11: the seismic cannon is replaced by the ship's helicopter. After the radio call the yellow console on the
bridge starts it; go up to the roof, board it, fly round Kthulu and shoot it — the head above all — with twin machine
guns while dodging its tentacles. Do not shoot the ship: she blows up. When Kthulu falls across the bow it gives back
the captain's body and a gold card; the helicopter lands itself, the card opens the autopilot room on the roof, and the
autopilot takes the ship past the iceberg to Polaris Station. Mechanical reload sounds for every weapon; no instructions
written on the ship (the Writer and on-screen messages say what to do); three scenery bugs fixed.

Languages: Italiano (default), English, Español, Français, Deutsch — Settings → Language.
PC: WASD · mouse · left mouse / R / Q / Enter fire · T reload · C crouch (X toggles) · Space jump · E use · 1–7 weapons.
Helicopter: WASD fly · Space climb · C descend · hold fire.
Phone / tablet: landscape, left thumb moves, right thumb looks, on-screen buttons (in the helicopter: JUMP climbs, DUCK descends).
