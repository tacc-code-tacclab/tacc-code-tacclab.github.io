# Bertranda 2 for Roblox Studio

Open `Bertranda2.rbxlx` in Roblox Studio and press Play.

The place contains the full 25-schema campaign: asylum, cemetery, spirit forest, horror castle and catacombs repeated through Verdigris, Ash, Violet, Rot and Inferno corruption cycles. Bertranda becomes stronger and more deformed in every cycle, with a final infernal fight in schema 25.

Controls:

- `WASD`: move
- Mouse: aim
- Left click or `Space`: fire
- `R`: reload
- `E` or `F`: toggle the weapon light
- Touch: on-screen movement, aiming and combat controls

The place is self-contained and uses procedural geometry, lighting, sound synthesis and interface elements. No external Roblox asset IDs are required.
