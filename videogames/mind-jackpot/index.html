Scientific debrief

The prespecified primary descriptive score is the total number of position-specific matches among 180 predictions. Under independent random targets, each prediction has probability 1/6. Exact three-symbol jackpots and confidence calibration are secondary outcomes.

Immediate feedback and arcade rewards may affect later choices and engagement; these sequential effects are recorded and should be analysed separately. This pilot is not evidence of individual precognition and is not a substitute for ethics approval, preregistration or independent replication.
