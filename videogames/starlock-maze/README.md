# Starlock Maze — One, Two, Three, Freeze!

An original neon maze-chase variation built around a red-light, green-light rule.

- Hold the arrow keys or touch D-pad to move; release every control to stop instantly.
- The opening screen explains movement, freezing, dot collection and Power Pearls.
- The Star Sentinel cycles through sleeping, warning and watching states.
- Early levels have long, calm sleep phases; later levels wake the Sentinel progressively more often.
- A strong full-screen flashing red warning precedes one louder alarm when the Sentinel opens its eyes.
- Sector 1 has one slow hunter, sector 2 has two, sector 3 has three, and later sectors add speed as well as pressure.
- Twelve level-specific palettes become progressively brighter and more fluorescent.
- Clearing sector 12 ends the campaign with a dedicated victory screen.
- Moving while the Sentinel is watching costs a life.
- Power Pearls temporarily let the runner eat hunters and fire with Space or X.
- Power shots destroy inner maze walls and eliminate hunters; outer walls remain solid.
- Procedural levels become progressively faster and less forgiving.
- Responsive 120 Hz simulation, independent enemies, mobile landscape controls, local records and procedural Web Audio.

This is a separate game and does not replace Neon Maze — Rift Runner.
