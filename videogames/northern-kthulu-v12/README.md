# The Northern Kthulu and the Silent Vessel — version 12

Self-contained build (`index.html`). Earlier versions stay where they are: `../northern-kthulu-v11/`,
`../northern-kthulu-v10/`, `../northern-kthulu/` (version 9).

New in 12: Nyarlathotep waits in the autopilot room and must be beaten on the wheelhouse roof before the autopilot can
be engaged; the autopilot room is full of racks, consoles and screens; engaging the autopilot no longer ends the game —
the ship gathers speed, Kthulu's body slides off the stove-in bow into a whirlpool, and she runs through the ice to
Polaris Station, where the game ends at the quay; the captain's body is a modelled human figure; the helicopter's guns
fire separate tracer rounds. Fixed: a breaker lever that came through the wall into the engine room, and the sea
surface rising through the lower decks in heavy weather.

Languages: Italiano (default), English, Español, Français, Deutsch — Settings → Language.
PC: WASD · mouse · left mouse / R / Q / Enter fire · T reload · C crouch (X toggles) · Space jump · E use · 1–7 weapons.
Helicopter: WASD fly · Space climb · C descend · hold fire.
Phone / tablet: landscape, left thumb moves, right thumb looks, on-screen buttons.
