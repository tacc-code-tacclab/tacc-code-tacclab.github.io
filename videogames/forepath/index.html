Scientific debrief and limitations

The primary descriptive outcome is the number of exact matches, with a fixed chance probability of 0.20 per trial. Confidence, response time, card preference and emotional valence are secondary measures.

A static browser pilot cannot prevent technically skilled participants from inspecting local memory, cannot guarantee independent participation, and does not replace ethics review or preregistration. Confirmatory analysis must use a predeclared sample size, exclusions and statistical model.
