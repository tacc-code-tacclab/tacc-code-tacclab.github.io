# Roblox Studio edition

1. Download `Bertranda.rbxlx`.
2. Open Roblox Studio and choose **File → Open from File…**.
3. Select the downloaded file.
4. Press **Play**. The server builds the brighter boarding-house arena, full-auto blaster, continuous swarm and Bertranda boss fight.
5. To publish it to your Roblox account, use **File → Publish to Roblox As…**.

## Controls and objective

- Standard Roblox movement (the direction controls are not remapped)
- Mouse or touchscreen to aim
- Hold left mouse or `Space` to fire
- `R` to reload
- `F` to toggle the strong weapon light
- Mobile includes dedicated FIRE and reload buttons

Aim at the human heads for extra damage and eliminate Bertranda. Cockroaches, bats and snakes spawn continuously, attack the players and explode into loud chitin fragments when destroyed.

## Human faces and sound

The place is self-contained: each creature has a three-dimensional human head with Roblox's built-in face texture, altered with red eyes, different proportions, skin and hair. The face decals are deliberately named `ReplaceWithHorrorFaceTexture`, so after uploading the four PNG files from the browser edition to your own Roblox account you can paste those asset IDs into Studio if you want exact visual parity.

Explosions and the original baroque-style pulse use Roblox-bundled sounds, not paid or third-party assets.
