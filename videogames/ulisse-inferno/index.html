FROM TROY TO ITHACA

Fourteen rooms.
One living myth.

Each single-screen chapter translates an episode of the Odyssey into a compact mechanical trap, with its own creatures, ships, waves and landmarks. Every element is drawn as integer pixel art.

Solo mode follows Odysseus. In two-player mode, Odysseus and Athena race locally on one keyboard; the first to the door earns the chapter.
