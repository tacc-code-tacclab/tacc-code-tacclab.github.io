# LIFEBEARER — Einstein's Drift

A lightweight landscape HTML5 game about special relativity, curved spacetime and panspermia.

- Leave the mothership and fly as a free astronaut.
- Avoid asteroids, comets, black holes and galactic gravity wells.
- The spacetime grid deforms around massive objects in real time.
- Velocity is expressed as a fraction of `c`; the Lorentz factor controls time dilation, length contraction and the growing high-energy collision field.
- Brake, approach the target world and seed life to unlock the next Solar System mission.
- Keyboard, touch arrows and an analogue mobile thumb stick are supported.

The generated transparent astronaut and mothership assets are stored in `assets/`; planets and spacetime effects are rendered procedurally.
