The cold takes you

The Skadi leans into the turn. The wall of ice slides down her port side close enough to touch, groans once along the plating, and falls astern.

Whatever carried her north is gone, sunk until its light was one more star under the water. For the first time in eleven days she is only a ship, and something is steering her.

On the floes the white bears stop, lift their heads, and watch you go.

Hours later the lights of Polaris Station rise out of the dark. The people on the ice dock ask about the crew. You tell them to check the sonar: one contact, very deep, heading north. Then eight more behind it.
