The cold takes you

The last pulse rolls out under the ice like a door closing. The thing that carried the Skadi north lets her go, coil by coil, and sinks until its light is one more star under the water.

For the first time in eleven days the ship is only drifting. On the floes, the figures that kept pace with her fold down, one after another, and are gone.

You hook on. The winch takes your weight. Nobody on the helicopter asks about the crew, and you do not tell them what the big ones were wearing.

Forty minutes south, the sonar operator logs one contact, very deep, heading north. Then eight more behind it.
